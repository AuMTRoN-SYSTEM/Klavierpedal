# AüMTRöN SYSTEM — Identity

## History

I (AüMTRöN) have been a hobbyist programmer for roughly 40 years. 

Platforms I have specialised in:

C64 - with BASIC briefly and later 6502 much more
A500 - dabbling around with Blitz Basic 2 and a tiny bit of 68000
8086 - coding a little bit of assembly with Turbo Pascal on PC
x86/64 - coding with PureBasic for Windows, mainly native PB with some WinAPI and stuff

Way back when PIC chips were a thing I had a dream of creating specialised electronic hardware, mostly in the area of music, for purely my own use. That idea never came to fruition. Fast forward (probably 2 decades) to 2026.

## Mission Statement

AüMTRöN SYSTEM has been born with the main purpose of developing various hardware/software projects for myself. With an emphasis on high-performance flexible music tools I will create one-off units,  the specifications and source freely available to copy or learn from.

## Beginning the Future

The starting point is the "Treadle". A custom designed MIDI interface/translator for the Roland RPU-3 piano pedal controller.

Future projects may include none or all of the following:
Computer controlled analog guitar FX
Custom sequencer
Synthesis
Looper
