# AüMTRöN SYSTEM — Identity

## History

I've been a hobbyist programmer for around 40 years.

Over that time I've worked on several platforms, including:

- **Commodore 64** – BASIC initially, followed by a much longer journey with 6502 assembly.
- **Amiga 500** – Blitz Basic 2 and a small amount of 68000 assembly.
- **IBM PC (8086)** – Turbo Pascal with a little x86 assembly.
- **Modern Windows (x86/x64)** – Primarily PureBasic, with occasional WinAPI development.

Back when PIC microcontrollers were becoming popular, I had a long-standing ambition to build dedicated electronic hardware, mainly for music, purely for my own use. That idea remained dormant for many years before finally becoming reality in 2026.

## Mission Statement

AüMTRöN SYSTEM exists to design and build hardware and software projects primarily for my own use.

The focus is on high-performance, flexible music tools that solve real problems encountered during performance or production. While most projects are intended as one-off builds, their designs, documentation, and source code are published openly so others are free to learn from them, modify them, or build their own versions.

## Beginning the Future

The first AüMTRöN SYSTEM project is **Treadle**: a custom-designed MIDI interface and translator for the Roland RPU-3 triple piano pedal.

Future projects may include:

- Computer-controlled analogue guitar effects
- Custom MIDI/CV sequencer
- Synthesizer hardware and software
- Looper
- ...and whatever comes next.
